### [SuperTokens Core](https://github.com/supertokens/supertokens-core)

**Description:** Open-source backend for session management and authentication (email/password, passwordless, social/SSO login, MFA). Exposes an HTTP API consumed by SDKs in various backend languages; self-hostable or usable via managed service.

**Keywords:** authentication, auth, session management, self-hosted, open-source, SSO, passwordless, MFA, Auth0 alternative

**Possible usage:** Evaluate as a self-hosted alternative to third-party auth providers (e.g. Auth0, Keycloak) for services needing session/auth management without vendor lock-in or per-user pricing.

### [microG GmsCore](https://github.com/microg/GmsCore)

**Description:** Free and open-source, privacy-friendly reimplementation of Google's Play Services (GMS) — implements the Google APIs that apps rely on (push notifications/FCM, location, maps, etc.) without the proprietary Google code or telemetry.

**Keywords:** android, degoogled, privacy, open-source, play-services, FCM, push-notifications, LineageOS, GrapheneOS alternative component, de-googling

**Possible usage:** Reference for running Android apps that depend on Google Play Services on de-googled/custom ROM devices, or as a case study in reverse-engineering and reimplementing a proprietary API surface.
